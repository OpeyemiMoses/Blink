Built with Zero Compromise on Security

Blink has undergone rigorous code audits and penetration reviews. Our architecture implements local transaction reconstruction, eliminating external injection vulnerabilities.

A Physical Blink brings Solana Actions into the tangible world. Instead of clicking a link on a web screen, you physically tap your mobile device against an NFC sticker or scan a live QR code on a counter, poster, or card to instantly execute an on-chain transaction.
While Blink is custom-tailored and optimized for the Solana Seeker device (supporting its hardware Seed Vault and high-DPI AMOLED panel), the APK runs smoothly on any Android phone (version 10+) equipped with standard NFC or a camera for live QR scanning.
When a merchant creates an action priced in US Dollars or USDC, customers choosing to pay with the native $SKR SPL token receive an automatic 10% discount calculated in real-time. The transaction automatically creates your recipient token account idempotently if it doesn't already exist.
